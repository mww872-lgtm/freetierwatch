What's the difference between Microsoft Copilot (free) and Copilot in Microsoft 365
Get help with Copilot
How can I use what's the difference?
Tell me how Copilot Chat works
Copilot is an AI assistant designed to help you complete tasks faster and more efficiently. It uses large language models (LLMs) to respond to your questions or prompts. Copilot is integrated into many different Microsoft apps and services including Microsoft 365 subscriptions, and you might need help understanding what works best for your needs and what you might already have access to.
This article describes the differences between the free version of Copilot, and the version of Copilot included in individual and business Microsoft 365 subscriptions.
Microsoft Copilot (free) - This version of Copilot is available free of cost and is ideal for general questions and answers, people trying AI for the first time, and web-based tasks. You can access Copilot in your web browser at copilot.cloud.microsoft, download as an app on your desktop or mobile device, or use directly in Copilot in Edge.
Copilot in eligible individual Microsoft 365 plans - Copilot is available in individual subscriptions such as Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, or Premium and subscribers can access Copilot Chat in their web browser at microsoft365.com or download the app to your mobile device. The subscription owner has access to Copilot in the desktop apps which includes deep integration with documents, emails, spreadsheets, calendars and more. Usage limits apply (AI credits and limits for Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro).
Copilot in Microsoft 365 business plans - Access to Copilot in eligible Microsoft 365 business plans depends on your type of license. Users with a business subscription have access to AI chat grounded on the web, pay-as-you-go agents, built-in IT controls, enterprise data protection, and agent management. Depending on how your organization is configured you might also have access to Copilot directly in Microsoft 365 apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote.
Learn more about each of these versions in the sections below.

Individual Microsoft 365 plans: Copilot in Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro
The Copilot features available in individual plans such as Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, or Premium depend on which subscription you have and if you're the subscription owner.
To access Copilot in Microsoft 365, sign in with your Microsoft account at microsoft365.com or download the app to your mobile device. Subscription owners can also access Copilot within the Microsoft 365 desktop apps.
Tip
If Microsoft 365 Family or Premium is shared with you, you can access Chat at microsoft365.com by signing in with the Microsoft account associated with your subscription. Other AI benefits, such as Copilot in the desktop apps are limited to the subscription owner.
Subscription owners of Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, or Premium have access to Copilot in the Microsoft 365 desktop apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneNote. AI usage limits apply. Learn more at Understanding AI Credits.
Microsoft 365 Premium users also have exclusive access to:
Advanced AI features
Agents like Tasks, Analyst, and Researcher.
Extended usage allowance (see Understanding AI Credits)
Learn more about Microsoft 365 Premium.
If your version of Microsoft 365 Family or Premium is through sharing, AI benefits such as Copilot in the desktop apps are limited to the subscription owner, but you can still access Chat at microsoft365.com by signing in with the Microsoft account associated with your subscription.
Not sure what subscription you have? Learn more in What Copilot license do I have.
Important
Copilot features in Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro subscriptions are subject to limits and can be used by subscription owner only. See Understanding AI Credits.
Learn more about Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro features and pricing.

Microsoft 365 business plans: Copilot Chat and Microsoft Copilot
What you can do and what you have access to with Copilot in Microsoft 365 business plans depends on how your organization is configured and the type of license your admin assigned you. Those with an eligible Microsoft 365 license will have access to Microsoft Copilot Chat which gives you secure, enterprise-ready AI Chat. Your admin might also choose to assign you a Microsoft Copilot add-on license which can help to increase employee productivity and streamline processes.
Microsoft Copilot Chat
Copilot Chat is automatically included and available to organizations with an eligible Microsoft 365 license. With Copilot Chat, you have:
AI chat grounded on the web and powered by the latest models
Ask questions and do research
Summarize documents and web pages
Create images
Use pay-as-you-go agents for task automation
Copilot Chat includes standard access to capabilities like file upload, image generation, and LLMs.
Microsoft Copilot
Microsoft Copilot is an add-on license which includes all the features in Copilot Chat along with advanced AI benefits such as:
AI-powered Chat that can respond using your work data like files, emails, chats, people, and more.
Included access to agents based on your work data and to reasoning agents like Researcher and Analyst
Advanced features both in-app and in Copilot Chat in Microsoft 365 apps
Meeting summaries and recaps in Microsoft Teams (if available)
Premium AI search and creation functionality
More security, privacy, and compliance features for IT admins
The Microsoft Copilot license includes priority access for faster response times and more consistent availability.
Learn more in How Copilot Chat works with and without a Microsoft Copilot license.

Frequently Asked Questions

01/
What is Microsoft Copilot and what does it do?
Microsoft Copilot is an AI-powered assistant designed to help you be more productive. It can help you create content, find information, summarize documents, emails, and meetings, analyze data, and get answers faster. Copilot works across Microsoft 365 experiences and integrates with apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams to help you save time and accomplish more.
02/
What is the difference between the Copilot mobile app and the Copilot experience within the Microsoft 365 apps?
The Copilot mobile app provides a conversational AI chat experience for asking questions, creating content, conducting research, and more. Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps brings AI-powered assistance directly into apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook, where it can help create, edit, summarize, analyze, and transform content within the context of your work.
03/
How do I use Copilot in Outlook, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and does it work across Microsoft 365 apps?
Copilot offers a range of capabilities to help make you more productive, including the ability to reference a file to create a document in Word or a presentation in PowerPoint. You can also generate images directly from the Copilot chat pane in Word and PowerPoint. As more people adopt Copilot, we'll continue enhancing how it works across Microsoft 365 apps to deliver an even more seamless experience.
04/
What are the most common use cases for Copilot in Microsoft 365?
Copilot supports a wide range of use cases, including creating content, conducting research, analyzing data, managing communications, and completing complex tasks across Microsoft 365 apps.
05/
Are there AI features in Copilot for writing, deep research, data analysis, creating presentations, and image editing?
Yes. Copilot can help you draft and refine content, research and gather information, analyze data and identify insights, create presentations, summarize content, and generate or edit images. These capabilities are integrated across Microsoft 365 experiences to help you complete tasks faster and with less manual effort.
06/
How can I manage my Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium or Pro subscription?
Manage your Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, or Pro subscription by signing into your Microsoft account and going to Services & Subscriptions. There, you can view or renew your subscription, update your payment method, manage billing settings, switch plans, and access your subscription benefits.
07/
I'm a Microsoft 365 Family subscriber. Does my family get access to Copilot as a part of our plan now?
While Microsoft 365 Family benefits such as apps and OneDrive storage can be shared, Copilot AI features are currently available only to the subscription owner and are not included for other family members. Learn more here.
08/
Are there any special offers for college students?
Yes. Eligible college students may qualify for special Microsoft education offers, including discounted Microsoft 365 subscriptions. Some students may also be eligible for free access to Microsoft 365 through their school or university. Students can check eligibility and available offers using a valid college or university email address on Microsoft's student offer page. Offers may vary by institution and region.
09/
What are the benefits of Copilot in Microsoft 365 compared to other AI tools?
With Copilot, you can conveniently tap into the power of AI in the Microsoft 365 apps you use every day, without the need to toggle between screens or tabs, making it a more seamless experience. Copilot can even apply formatting to your content in the Microsoft 365 apps, taking advantage of hundreds of features many people rarely use. And users can share and collaborate in real time on content generated by Copilot in apps like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
10/
Is Copilot secure? How does Microsoft use my data and protect my privacy?
Copilot is built on Microsoft's existing security, privacy, compliance, and Responsible AI commitments. Learn more here.
